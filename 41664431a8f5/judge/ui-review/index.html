Review a UI with the judge

View as MarkdownOpen in ClaudeOpen in ChatGPT

This tutorial reviews Orchard’s battle HUD in about 30 to 45 minutes instead of hours. Scion captures 20 HUD states at two resolutions, the gates catch objective problems, the judge scores and ranks every capture, an agent reviews only the worst and the uncertain ones next to reference screens, makes variations, the judge compares them in pairs, and a person picks between the final two. The vote trains the judge for next time.

Before you begin

Section titled “Before you begin”
  • The HudStates static trial (Capture every HUD state).
  • Trained heads readability, has_glitch and looks_like_orchard (Train a head).
  • Optional: a reference library of screens from other games, indexed by the judge (scion/judge/references/).

Steps

Section titled “Steps”

1. Capture (about 1 minute)

Section titled “1. Capture (about 1 minute)”

40 stills, two sheets.

2. Gates (seconds)

Section titled “2. Gates (seconds)”

HudStates enables TextOverflow, TextContrast and UiGrid. Failures come first in trial show --failed.

3. Judge (seconds)

Section titled “3. Judge (seconds)”

Every capture gets a score per head; low-confidence answers are listed under escalate.

4. References (milliseconds)

Section titled “4. References (milliseconds)”

For each capture, the five most similar reference screens and their notes.

5. Agent review of the worst (5 to 10 minutes)

Section titled “5. Agent review of the worst (5 to 10 minutes)”

The agent reads only the 10 to 15 lowest-scored and escalated captures, each next to its references, and writes a short list of problems with evidence (state, region).

6. Variations (5 to 15 minutes)

Section titled “6. Variations (5 to 15 minutes)”

The agent writes graft or theme changes for the problems (spacing, contrast, icon size) as drafts and runs the states again with --grafts.

7. Re-judge in pairs (3 to 5 minutes)

Section titled “7. Re-judge in pairs (3 to 5 minutes)”

8. A person picks (about 5 minutes)

Section titled “8. A person picks (about 5 minutes)”

The person sees the top two variants per state, blind, and picks.

9. The vote trains

Section titled “9. The vote trains”

Check the result

Section titled “Check the result”
  • trial show HudStates --failed lists no gate failures.
  • judge run scores the winning variant above the original on readability for most states.
  • judge show readability reports a new version, promoted, with agreement on the gold set equal or higher.

Next steps

Section titled “Next steps”
  • Promote the winning grafts and approve the new stills (From draft to final build).
  • Add the HUD review to CI with only steps 1 and 2 (Run Scion in CI).
  • Train an audience head for accessibility (Taste heads and feel presets).
